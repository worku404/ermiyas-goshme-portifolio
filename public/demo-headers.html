Option 1: Floating Glass Capsule (Floating Pill)

Why It Works: Detaches the navigation from the browser window edge, letting the background architectural video and grid flow freely above and around the pill. It creates high-end visual depth through frosted glass (16px blur) and consolidates utility buttons into a compact cluster.

Button Hierarchy: The verbose Theme: light (light) button is streamlined into a minimal 🌓 icon, language is compacted into EN | አም, and CV ↓ is a subtle jewel CTA.

Option 2: Smart Auto-Hide Edge Bar (Headroom)

Why It Works: When the visitor scrolls down to view blueprints, models, and sketches, the header glides smoothly off the top of the screen (0% obstruction). The second the visitor scrolls up even 10px, it glides back down instantly for effortless navigation.

Option 3: Drafting Title-Block & Corner Anchors
